⚡ THE CHALLENGE

Mist Systems, a fast-growing AI networking startup, needed a more professional brand to sell into the enterprise. Then Juniper Networks acquired Mist, requiring a full integration of product brand into corporate brand without disruption to either business.

🎯 THE APPROACH

Built a new enterprise-ready brand for Mist, then led the product-brand integration post-acquisition, ensuring "Juniper driven by Mist AI" felt like a coherent brand promise, not a stapled-together acquisition. The "Juniper driven by Mist AI" brand launched with a unified digital and physical presence. The integration preserved Mist's brand equity while elevating it within the Juniper family, setting a template for how product and corporate brand can coexist and strengthen each other.
